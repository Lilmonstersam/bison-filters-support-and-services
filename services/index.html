The Services

Three services that cut labour, not corners

Built for service managers and coordinators quoting maintenance contracts at scale, and facility managers running multiple sites from one register.

01

Filter kitting: the right filters, marked for the right unit

Send us your site list and we bundle every change-out into unit-specific kits. Each kit is labelled by unit number and matched to your filter register, so when a large order lands on site your techs know exactly what goes in what unit. For multi-site contractors, kitting standardises the job across every location and every crew.

Delivery & drop-off: to site, and away again

In Adelaide and Melbourne metro, add drop-off collection and we remove your old filters for compliant disposal. One delivery, one collection, zero double-handling of spent media.

Replacement reminders: preventative maintenance on autopilot
